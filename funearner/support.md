# FunEarner — Support

_Last updated: 2 August 2026_

Need help with FunEarner? You're in the right place.

## Quick answers

**The app asks for Screen Time authorization — is that safe?**
Yes. FunEarner uses Apple's Family Controls framework, which is sandboxed. FunEarner never sees the raw list of apps your child used; it only receives the aggregated signals it needs to unlock rewards.

**Where is my data stored?**
Everything stays on your device. There is no cloud backend, no account, no login.

**How do I remove all data?**
Uninstalling the app erases all local data.

**A shield/limit isn't triggering.**
Make sure Screen Time authorization is granted in **Settings → Screen Time → Share Across Devices / Family** and that FunEarner is allowed. Restart the device if a rule was just added.

## Contact

Send an email to [itunes@allamand.com](mailto:itunes@allamand.com) with:
- Your device model and iOS version
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots if relevant

We usually reply within a few working days.
